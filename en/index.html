Terra Vegan: self-production is freedom

Terra Vegan is a journey, not a recipe collection.

It was born from the conviction that cooking is an act of freedom: freedom to choose what comes to the table, to reduce dependence on industrial distribution, to produce with one's own hands food that is ethical, healthy and extraordinarily good.

40 volumes in four series accompany anyone from everyday cooking to certified professional artisan production. It begins with a bruschetta. It can lead, if desired, to HACCP and ISO 22000 certified production. The journey is yours: Terra Vegan gives you the tools to walk it.

No imitation, no compromise. Every preparation has its own dignity. Every recipe is a foundation from which to build, experiment and reinvent.

Plant-based cuisine is not deprivation. It is discovery.

Nemo, author of Terra Vegan

The author

Nemo

Independent author and researcher in the field of vegan self-production. His works combine technical expertise with an ethical approach: no industrial ingredients, focus on fermentation, traditional craftsmanship and sustainability.

Education: Diploma in Agricultural Science (Florence), Degree in Political Science (University of Florence)

Lead Auditor ISO 22000:2018 and ISO 9001:2015 · HACCP self-monitoring plan manager · Solid background in certified organic farming

Advanced vegan cuisine training: Vegan Basics, PlantStart, Vegan Fine Dining
