What the critics say about the Terra Vegan manuals

We are not the only judges of these manuals. The American critic Anthony Avina is reviewing the volumes of the English edition of Terra Vegan by Nemo one by one: here are his verdicts, in full.

Foundation Series · Vol. 1-9
★★★★★ 9 of 9 volumes reviewed · all 5 stars · Anthony Avina

Vol. 1 · Vegan Flavours of Italy

Review of 1 September 2026 · rating 10/10 · ★★★★★ · Anthony Avina, USA
the post on the blog · the Amazon page · the Goodreads page

This cookbook was fascinating and compelling. The author does a fantastic job capturing the heart and tradition of Italian cuisine while also exploring the history and need for vegan cooking in our modern world, as well as the various preparation methods and why certain ingredients interact in each recipe. The detail and variety of recipes came to life beautifully in this book.

But what really stood out was how the author developed and curated each recipe by grouping them into sections that fit not just a specific meal, but the natural flow of a meal in any Italian home or restaurant. From small nibbles and appetizers to big dinners or supper, each recipe added depth and a sense of heart and home that many associate with Italian cuisine. A personal favorite of mine opens the book with Mediterranean bruschetta, showing how simple, powerful, flavorful ingredients can come together without any meat-based products.

Thoughtfully curated, heartfelt, and engaging, author NEMO’s “Vegan Flavors of Italy” is a must-read cookbook and a great way to start off a fantastic series of reviews this month of this compelling series of vegan cookbooks centered around Italian cuisine. The depth of detail and the way these recipes were prepared complement the unique ingredients native to various regions in Italy and the commitment to veganism this cookbook promises.

Vol. 2 · The Essence of the Earth: Vegan Truffle Recipes

Review of 2 September 2026 · rating 10/10 · ★★★★★ · Anthony Avina, USA
the post on the blog · the Amazon page · the Goodreads page

This was a fantastic, insightful look at the many ways to incorporate truffles into everyday cuisine. These vegan recipes are inspired, and the depth of detail, and each recipe is perfectly balanced with the author’s ability to hone in on not only the ways in which the truffles are compatible with each recipe and the specific senses that will be triggered when consuming these recipes, but also pairs them perfectly with each season to get the most out of each truffle.

A standout moment in the book is how the author provides gluten-free alternatives for these recipes. While some may not recognize the need for gluten-free food, as it has become more trendy in recent years, as someone with family who has celiac disease, it is important that gluten-free food be more readily available, and the author’s attention to this detail is a welcome addition. One recipe that looked especially delicious was the mushroom and truffle burger; seeing how these vegan patties were prepared and how creamy the truffles were made it sound even more appealing.

Insightful, engaging, and delightful, “The Essence of the Earth” BY NEMO is a must-read Italian cuisine cookbook that emphasizes truffles. The vast number of recipes, as well as the way the author divides these recipes- not only by season and the specific truffles used with the recipe, but also by the types of recipes that these encompass, from finger foods to complex yet divine dinners, make this a great book to reference to use time and time again for anyone hoping to prepare authentic Italian cuisine using delicious vegan recipes.

The recipes found in this book are unique, exploring how Kome-Koji is used for Koji Water and Koji Paste, as well as fresh Koji and Amazake, and laying the foundation for future books in this series as Koji becomes the base of future topics.

Interesting, engaging, and memorable, author NEMO’s “Koji: Production and Applications” is a must-read nonfiction cookbook for vegan Italian cuisine. The book’s unique balance of history and biology, and its scientific process for bringing this base to life for future recipes, make for a remarkable read that pushes readers to better understand the ingredients they put into their bodies and what the natural world creates.

Vol. 19 · Scientific Safety: Metrology and Home Microbiology

Review of 6 October 2026 · rating 10/10 · ★★★★★ · Anthony Avina, USA
the post on the blog · the Amazon page · the Goodreads page

One aspect of cookbooks that people rarely touch upon that this one does a great job of, is honing in on the process and the need to understand safety in the kitchen. Not only is safety with various cooking equipment vital, but making sure the food that is consumed is safe to eat is equally important, and when developing vegan cheeses and other recipes, it is so crucial to understand that the proper parameters surrounding the development of these foods need to be followed to the letter so that the final product is safe enough to consume.

The author uses this book to break down the need for food safety and how that safety is measured. Measuring both pH levels and water retention, the author breaks down how each of these is properly measured by you as you cook, what you need to look for when testing these products, the proper tools that need to be utilized to test the safety of these foods, and finally the protocols that need to be followed, broken down by food type, from fermented foods in vegan meats and cheeses to sauces and so much more.

Compelling, insightful, and engaging, Author Nemo’s “Scientific Safety” is a must-read non-fiction vegan cookbook. The detail and knowledge readers gain from the author’s work on food safety in this book are impressive. Safety is vital, and it helps newcomers to vegan cooking understand what goes into these products and how safety is measured when developing these recipes.
